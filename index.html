About PHYKonnet Africa

Who We Are

PHYKonnet Africa is a community connecting physicists across Africa to learn, collaborate and grow.

🎯

Our Mission

To connect and empower physicists across Africa through collaboration, mentorship, knowledge sharing and opportunities.

◇

Our Vision

A connected African physics community contributing to scientific advancement, innovation and meaningful impact.

✦

Our Values

Collaboration, excellence, inclusivity, integrity and meaningful impact.
